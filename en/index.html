About

Between statistical rigour and curiosity for AI.

After a Bac D (science-oriented baccalaureate) and two years of preparatory classes, I joined ISSEA for the statistician-economist engineering programme (ISE). Passionate about programming, I work there on statistics, quantitative economics and applied data analysis. I have also co-founded, with fellow students, SONGRE CONSULTING, a firm that supports SMEs in data and digitalisation.

I like to connect these foundations with programming, survey practice, data entry, dashboards, real-time data pipelines and conversational assistants based on document retrieval (RAG). Each project is a chance for me to move from theory to a concrete tool, and I remain open to any cooperation.
